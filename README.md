# Dunga Tech & Security

Responsive static website starter for a South African security, networking and IT shop.

## Included
- Product catalogue with working category filters
- CCTV, gate automation, alarms, electric fencing, access control, backup power, networking and smart-home categories
- IT and technician-focused Tech Corner
- Services, about and customer enquiry sections
- Responsive mobile navigation
- Enquiry form that prepares an email in the visitor's email app

## Before publishing
1. In `script.js`, replace `yourbusiness@email.com` with your real business email.
2. Add your phone number, WhatsApp link, service area, address (if applicable), social links and business hours.
3. Confirm which products, brands and services you actually stock or provide. The catalogue is illustrative and does not show live stock.
4. Do not claim authorised reseller or installer status unless that is accurate.

## Publish using GitHub Pages
1. Sign in to GitHub and create a repository, for example `dunga-tech-security`.
2. Upload `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js` and `README.md` to the repository root.
3. Open **Settings → Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Select branch **main** and folder **/(root)**, then click **Save**.
6. Your published address will appear in the Pages section, usually `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/dunga-tech-security/`.

## Important
This is a static front-end site. It does not include live stock, online payments, customer accounts or server-side form handling. The enquiry form uses `mailto:` and opens the visitor's email application. For a proper online form, connect a form service or backend.
